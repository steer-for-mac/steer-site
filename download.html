Download Steer

Your licence key is in your email receipt. Download the app, paste the key in, and it's yours to keep.

Get the app

Steer hasn't shipped its first release yet. If you're waiting on it, join the launch list and you'll hear the day it's out.

Join the launch list

Activate

Open Steer, go to Settings → General, paste your licence key, and click Activate. That's it. The key works on every Mac you personally use, with no limit on how many.

Updates

Steer checks for updates only when you ask it to: Check for Updates in the menu bar. You never need to come back here for a new version.

Requirements

macOS 14 or later, on an Apple Silicon Mac. A DualSense, DualShock 4, Xbox, Switch Pro, or MFi controller.

Something wrong?

If the key won't activate, or the download won't open, email steer@seanfloyd.dev with the address you bought with. It's one person reading it, and it gets sorted.
