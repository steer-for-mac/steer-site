What’s in it →

Activate

Open Steer, go to Settings → General, paste your licence key, and click Activate. That's it. The key works on every Mac you use, at home or at work, with no limit on how many.

Updates

While "Receive beta updates" is on, which it is in the beta, Steer installs each newer build on its own. Turn it off and Steer checks only when you ask: Check for Updates in the menu bar. Either way, you never need to come back here for a new version.

Requirements

macOS 14 or later, on an Apple Silicon Mac. A DualSense, DualShock 4, Xbox, Switch Pro, or MFi controller.

Something wrong?

If the key won't activate, or the download won't open, email steer@seanfloyd.dev with the address you bought with. It's one person reading it, and it gets sorted.
