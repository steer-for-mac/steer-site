What it does.

Pictures are the app with a DualSense; button names follow your controller.

{{ shot("help", 2078, 1500, "Steer’s help card: a DualSense with every control labelled. Cross is Left Click, the left stick moves the mouse, pressing the right stick opens the on-screen keyboard.") }}

Every button already has a job.

Plug it in: point, click, scroll, switch apps. Press Create (left of the touchpad) and the help card shows every button for the app in front. Hold a shoulder button and the rest change jobs until you let go. Like Shift.

{{ loop("keyboard", 1600, 848, "Steer’s on-screen keyboard: a full Mac layout split in two, with F and J lit where each stick is aiming.", 2354, 1248) }}

A Mac keyboard, split for two thumbs.

Each stick aims at one half; the triggers type. Space, Delete and Return are face buttons. Or press the left stick and say it.

{{ shot("apps", 1714, 873, "Steer’s Gallery: ready-made setups for Final Cut Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Lightroom Classic, drawing apps, Keynote, Terminal, Xcode and code editors.") }}

Every app gets its own buttons.

Add Final Cut Pro, Lightroom Classic or Keynote from the Gallery. Its buttons switch in when you open the app, and out when you leave.

{{ loop("ring", 704, 838, "Steer’s ring of apps: Safari, Finder, Music, Mail, Calendar, Notes, Messages, Settings and Maps, with Aim, Select and Cancel below.", 704, 838) }}

A ring of apps.

Press the left stick, point, press ✕. Make your own rings for anything else.

{{ loop("snap", 1120, 1102, "Steer’s Window Snap: nine places for a window, from Top Left to Bottom Right, with Centre picked.", 1036, 795) }}

Window Snap.

Nine places on the screen. Pick one; the window goes there. Uses Rectangle, Raycast, or the Mac’s own tiling on macOS 15 and later.

{{ shot("tiles", 1817, 203, "Five settings tiles: Button Combos, Gyro, Lights, Trigger Resistance and Vibration.") }}

Triggers that push back.

On a DualSense the triggers resist as you press. Vibration when Steer acts, a light colour per set of jobs, tilt to aim. To change a button, click it on the picture in Settings; History keeps restore points if you change your mind.
