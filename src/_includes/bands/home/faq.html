Questions.

Will it work with my controller?

DualSense, DualShock 4, Xbox and Switch Pro are supported directly, with light colours and trigger resistance where the controller has them, and 300+ others through a shared database of controller layouts. Button names follow your controller, so an Xbox shows A and B, not ✕ and ○. Every controller, in detail.

Do I have to set it all up?

No. It works when you plug in, and a short setup teaches you with the controller in hand.

My phone is already a remote. Why this?

A remote points and clicks. Steer also types, switches buttons per app, and gives every button a second job.

Is it safe to give it permission?

Steer asks for one Mac permission, Accessibility, which is what lets any app send clicks and keys. A DualSense Edge’s back paddles also need Input Monitoring. There’s no account and no analytics. It goes online to check your licence (it sends the key, nothing about you), to look for updates when you ask, and to fetch a setup you import by link. What it can and can’t touch.

How is it different from other controller apps?

It types on a full Mac keyboard, every button has a second job while you hold a shoulder, and each app can have its own setup, ready-made in the Gallery. The compare page goes point by point, with sources.

Is it a subscription?

No. When it ships it’s €19.99 once, after a 14-day trial with no card and no account.
