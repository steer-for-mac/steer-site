A layout for the app you are in.

Steer ships ready-made layouts for Mail, your browser, Final Cut Pro and more. Add the ones you want, and it switches to them on its own whenever that app comes to the front.

02Browser

  1. Right stick: scroll the page

  3. L2 + D-pad right: forward

03Final Cut Pro

  1. L1 + L2 and L1 + R2: shuttle back and forward
  2. R2 + Triangle: blade at the playhead
  3. R2 + Circle: undo
  4. R2 + D-pad up and down: zoom the timeline

04Mail

  1. D-pad: move through your messages
  2. L1 + Cross: new message
  3. R2 + Triangle: reply
  4. R2 + Square: reply to all
