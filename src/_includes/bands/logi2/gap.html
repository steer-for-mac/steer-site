Why a controller

Buttons you find without looking. Jobs that change with the app.

Your thumbs know where every button is, so your eyes stay on the screen. Steer gives each button a job, and it works the moment you plug in.

Give an app its own layout, and the moment that app is in front, the same buttons take on its jobs. Change any of them whenever you like.

macOS's own Game Controllers pane only swaps one button for another, inside games. Checked on macOS 27.
