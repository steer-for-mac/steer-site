Start on the sofa. Keep going at the desk.

  • Movie night

    Leave the keyboard where it is: the Browser preset puts play, pause, skip ahead and full screen on the controller.

  • Family photos

    Everyone stays on the sofa: the D-pad is the arrow keys, so Photos steps from one picture to the next.

  • In bed

    Stay lying down: the right stick scrolls the page, and the Browser preset pauses the video.

  • Video editing

    Sit back from the timeline: the Final Cut Pro and DaVinci Resolve presets put playback, marks and cuts on the controller.

  • Vibe coding

    Lean back while an AI writes the code: dictate what you want, send it with one button, and your controller can buzz when it's done (one line in your agent's settings).

  • Presenting

    Walk around while you present: the left stick moves the pointer on the big screen.
