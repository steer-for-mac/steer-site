Switch apps, and the buttons switch with you.

Steer comes with one layout that works everywhere, and ready-made ones for your browser, Mail, Final Cut Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Figma, Xcode and more. Add one in Presets. Pick one below to see what it puts on your controller.

Each layout has layers, which work like Shift on a keyboard: hold a shoulder button and the other buttons take on a second job. The one that works everywhere already has five, from copy and paste to find.
