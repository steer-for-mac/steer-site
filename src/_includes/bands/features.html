What else you get.

Six more surfaces, captured from the app and named the way the app names them. No renders, no touch-ups.

On-Screen Keyboard

Aim the stick at a slice of letters and press the face button under the one you want. It types into whatever has the cursor.

Radial Menu

Your apps in a ring at the tip of your thumb. Aim, press, and you are in the next one.

Help Overlay

Every button's job for the app you are in, on one card. Hold a shoulder button and its column lights up.

Menu bar

Steer lives in the menu bar: which controller is on, its battery, the layout in use, and the setup wizard if you ever want it.

Import review

Bring in someone else's setup, and anything that opens a page or runs a script is shown to you first. You approve it, or it does not run.

Presets

Ready-made layouts for your browser, Mail, Final Cut Pro, DaVinci Resolve, the Figma design app and the Cursor code editor. Add one, then change what you like.
