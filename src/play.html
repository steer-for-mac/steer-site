Steer, in your browser

Plug in a controller. This page is the demo.

Steer turns a game controller into a mouse and keyboard for your Mac. This page runs its default layout, so you can try it before it ships with a PlayStation, Xbox or Switch Pro controller.

What is real here, and what isn't

The same as the app

Every button does what it does in Steer's default setup: the same letters in the same places on the keyboard, the same apps in the ring, the same help card. Hold a shoulder button and the other buttons change jobs, exactly as they do on your Mac.

Only on your Mac

A web page can't open Spotlight, switch apps or launch Safari. When you press a button that would, the bar at the top names what it does instead. Picking an app in the ring names it rather than opening it.

Your controller stays here

Your browser reads the controller inside this tab. Nothing you press or type is sent anywhere. In Safari, press a button once so the page can see the controller.
