What is real here, and what isn't

The same as the app

Every button does what it does in Steer's own setup: the same split keyboard, the same apps in the ring, the same help card. Hold a shoulder button and the other buttons change jobs, exactly as they do on your Mac.

Only on your Mac

A web page can't open Spotlight, switch apps or launch Safari. When you press a button that would, the line above the demo names what it does instead. Picking an app in the ring names it rather than opening it.

Your controller stays here

Your browser reads the controller inside this tab. Nothing you press or type is sent anywhere. In Safari, press a button once so the page can see the controller.
