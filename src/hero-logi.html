Get off the desk

Take the wheel

Take control of your Mac from wherever you are sitting, with the controller you already own. Point and click, scroll, type, switch apps and run saved tasks. Steer comes with a working layout, so it does something useful the moment you plug in, and you can change any of it later.

macOS 14+ and Apple Silicon. Works with 315 controllers.

App specific customizations

Steer is customizable in every app you use. Work faster with ready-made layouts, or create your own for your favourite apps such as Safari, Final Cut Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Keynote and Xcode.

Default

  1. Left stick: move the pointer
  2. Right stick: scroll
  3. Cross: click
  4. R1: Spotlight

Safari

  1. Right stick: scroll the page
  2. D-pad left: back
  3. D-pad right: forward
  4. R1: next tab

Video editing

  1. L2 and R2: scrub
  2. Cross: cut
  3. Square: undo
  4. Right stick: timeline

Slides

  1. R1: next slide
  2. L1: previous slide
  3. Right stick: pointer dot
  4. Circle: exit

CHECK BEFORE SHIPPING: every profile named here has to exist in the app. Only Default is read out of Defaults.swift; the other three are inferred from the use-case cards.
